// about

The native Android specialist
who crossed platforms — on purpose.

I started writing Android apps when Kotlin was still a beta experiment. Over nine years of shipping production software, I've built everything from fintech dashboards and healthcare trackers to logistics platforms and e-commerce apps — each one with the performance discipline and architecture depth that only comes from working close to the metal.

When Flutter matured enough to meet my standards, I added it to my toolkit. Not as a shortcut, but as a force multiplier: one carefully-crafted codebase, genuinely native experience on both platforms. The clients I work with don't get a "good enough" cross-platform build — they get Android-quality thinking applied to every screen, on every OS.

I work best with founders and product teams who care about shipping software that feels right, not just software that technically ships. If that sounds like you, let's talk.
